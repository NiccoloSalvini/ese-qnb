## Part 1 — Drill set (paper, show all work)

**H1.** €12,000 is invested for 5 years at 7%.
(a) Final amount with simple interest. (b) With annual compounding. (c) The difference, and one line on where it comes from.

**H2.** €20,000 at 6% nominal for 3 years, compounded (a) annually (b) quarterly (c) monthly. Give the three final amounts.

**H3 (EAR).** Compute the effective annual rate of 9% nominal compounded (a) semi-annually (b) quarterly (c) monthly (d) continuously. Then say in one line why a borrower should always ask for the EAR.

**H4 (resit form).** *Select the correct alternative and justify in one line.*
Two loans quote the same nominal rate. Loan A compounds monthly, loan B annually. For the borrower:
(i) A is cheaper (ii) B is cheaper (iii) they cost the same

**H5 (present value).**
(a) What is €25,000 receivable in 6 years worth today at 8%?
(b) How much must be invested today at 5% to have €50,000 in 10 years?

**H6 (annuity).** A lease requires €9,000 at the end of each year for 7 years. Your cost of capital is 6%.
(a) The present value of the lease.
(b) The seller offers to sell the asset outright for €52,000. Which is cheaper in today's money, and by how much?

**H7 (perpetuity).** A building is let for €36,000 a year, for ever, first payment in one year.
(a) Its value at a 6% required return. (b) At 8%. (c) One line on why the two differ so much.

**H8 (NPV — the decision).** A project costs €900,000 now and returns €260,000, €300,000, €340,000 and €360,000 at the end of the next four years. The cost of capital is 10%.
(a) Lay out a table: year, flow, discount factor, present value.
(b) The NPV.
(c) Accept or reject, in one sentence.
(d) Recompute at 18%. What has changed, and what is the name of the rate between the two where the sign flips?

## Part 2 — Memo to the Board (150–250 words, typed)

The Board of Arno Bikes has been offered the assembly line from class: **€2.4M now, returning €700k, €800k, €900k and €1,000k over four years.** A Board member says: *"It returns €3.4M on a €2.4M outlay. That is a million of profit — obviously we do it."*

Write the memo that corrects this, politely and with numbers. Say what is wrong with adding the flows up, give the NPV at the firm's 9% cost of capital, and state the rate at which the answer would change. End with a recommendation and one condition you would attach to it.

No formulas in the text. Put the discounting table in an appendix if you want it seen.

## Self-check before submitting

- Every compound-interest answer states `m` (how many times a year) explicitly.
- Every EAR is computed from the nominal rate, never compared to it directly.
- Every present value shows the discount factor separately from the flow.
- The NPV table has a row for year 0, with a factor of 1.
- The memo names the number that makes the Board member's reasoning wrong, and does it in one clause.
