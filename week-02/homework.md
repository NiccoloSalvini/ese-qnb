## Part 1 — Drill set (paper, show all work)

**H1.** State the domain of each, as an interval, and say in one clause what the restriction means for a firm.
(a) `f(q) = √(2q − 30)`  (b) `g(q) = ln(40 − q)`  (c) `h(q) = (q + 5)/(q² − 9)`

**H2.** A workshop has `C(q) = 0.4q² + 8q + 200` and sells at a fixed price of €60.
(a) Write `R(q)` and `π(q)`.
(b) Evaluate `π(30)`, `π(65)`, `π(100)`.
(c) From those three numbers alone, between which two quantities must the best output lie? Say how you know, without computing it.

**H3.** Evaluate, showing the factoring step:
(a) `lim (q→4) (q² − 16)/(q − 4)`
(b) `lim (q→2) (q² − q − 2)/(q − 2)`
(c) `lim (q→0⁺) (q² + 3q)/q`

**H4 (exam form).** Evaluate `lim (q→2⁺) (q − 2)²/(2q − q²)`. Write out the factoring and comment on the sign of each factor as `q` approaches 2 from above.

**H5.** Evaluate and give the business reading of each:
(a) `lim (q→∞) (0.4q² + 8q + 200)/q` — average cost per unit as the run gets long. Why does this one *not* settle down, and what does that say about the workshop?
(b) `lim (q→∞) (200 + 15q)/q`.

**H6.** Differentiate:
(a) `π(q) = −3q² + 900q − 40,000`  (b) `C(q) = 0.4q² + 8q + 200`
(c) `R(q) = 45 ln(q + 1)`  (d) `f(q) = 2q⁴ − 6q² + q − 9`

**H7.** For `π(q) = −3q² + 900q − 40,000`:
(a) Compute `π'(50)` and write the one sentence you would say to the owner.
(b) Compute `π'(200)` and do the same.
(c) Solve `π'(q) = 0` and state what that quantity is.

**H8 (exam form).** Find `f(q)` given `f'(q) = 9q² − 4q + 2` and `f(1) = 10`.

## Part 2 — Memo to the Board (150–250 words, typed)

You are the plant manager of **Arno Bikes**. Last month you produced 80 bikes. The Board has asked for one number: **should the next bike be built?**

Answer it with the derivative. Give the marginal profit at 80, say what it means in euros, and state the quantity at which the answer changes from yes to no. Then add one sentence on what you would need to check before trusting the number — the model says demand is `p = 1600 − 4q` and cost is `60,000 + 400q`; say which of those two you would verify first, and why.

Numbers and their meaning. No formulas in the text; put the working in an appendix if you want it seen.
