### 9. Step by step on a function you know (8')

Hold the chalk; they dictate. Uncover the steps one at a time if you can:
1. Domain [0, ∞) — "a quantity".
2. Limits: π(0) = −60,000, the rent with nothing sold; → −∞ far out. "No asymptote: a polynomial has none."
3. Range (−∞, 30,000].
4. Sign: zeros 63.4 and 236.6; positive between — "the profitable window".
5. π′ = 0 at 150; π″ = −8 < 0 → **maximum**, €30,000.
6. Concave everywhere, no inflection.
7. Sketch, with all of the above on it.

**Board sentence: "f′ finds the candidates; f″ says which kind. Writing 'maximum' without the second
derivative earns nothing."** And: **"The sketch is step seven. A sketch drawn first is a guess with a ruler."**
(Say "sketch last" three times this morning.)

### 10. Limits at the edge: asymptotes (4')

"Step 2 asks for limits, and limits are how you find asymptotes — lines the curve approaches and never
meets. The resit asks for them by name."
Vertical: "look at the edge of the domain": 7 ln(q+3) → −∞ as q → −3⁺, so q = −3.
Horizontal: "look at infinity": 0.4q + 3 + 25/q grows without bound, no asymptote; (60,000 + 400q)/q
→ 400, so y = 400 is one — "last week's floor".
**"A sketch with an asymptote missing is marked incomplete."**

### 11. The second derivative, in one line (2')

**"Positive holds water — convex, a minimum. Negative spills it — concave, a maximum."** Gesture a cup.
"An inflection is where the bending changes. In a report, it's the moment growth stops accelerating
and starts to slow — worth naming."

### 16. It is at the minimum, and that is not a coincidence (5')

Compute AC′ = 0 → q ≈ 7.91, AC = 9.32. [click] "And the marginal cost there: 9.32. **Equal.**"
Then the reason, in words — say it slowly: **"While the next unit costs less than the average, it pulls
the average down. When it costs more, it pushes it up. The average stops falling exactly when the two meet."**
Analogy if needed: a class average and the next student's mark.

### 17. The rule that runs every firm (6')

π′ = 0 ⇔ R′ = C′. "Say it in English before I do." → **"Produce up to the point where what one more
unit brings in equals what it costs."**
[click] The log-revenue firm. Clear the fraction **slowly on the board**: multiply both sides by (q+1),
expand, move everything to one side, multiply by 5 → 4q² + 19q − 135 = 0 → q ≈ 3.90 (keep the positive
root). "This clearing step is where good candidates slip under exam pressure."

### 18. LAB — push the log revenue (8')
