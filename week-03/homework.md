# Homework 3 — bring it to the next session

**Bring it to class, done on paper.** Nothing to hand in or send: the first 15–20 minutes of next session are spent going through it together at the board — you walk us through your working, we correct it there and then. Mistakes are the useful part, so bring what you tried even where you got stuck.

Two parts. Part 1 on paper. Part 2 typed. Expect 2–3 hours. This is the closest rehearsal of the midterm you will get before writing it, so treat Part 2 as a dry run.

## Part 1 — Drill set (paper, show all work)

**H1 (the full study).** Study `π(q) = −2q² + 640q − 24,000` completely and sketch it.
Give, in this order: domain · zeros · sign line · `π'` and the stationary point · `π''` and its nature · behaviour as `q → ∞` · a labelled sketch with all four key points marked.

**H2.** `C(q) = 0.2q² + 4q + 80` (€ thousand), `q ≥ 0`.
(a) Fixed cost.
(b) Average cost `AC(q)`, its domain, and its limits as `q → 0⁺` and `q → ∞`.
(c) The quantity that minimises `AC`, and the value of `AC` and of `C'` there. Comment in one line on what you find.

**H3 (optimisation).** A firm has `R(q) = 40 ln(q + 1)` and `C(q) = 0.2q² + 4q + 80`, both € thousand.
(a) Write `π(q)` and `π'(q)`.
(b) Solve `π'(q) = 0`, showing the quadratic it reduces to.
(c) Confirm the nature of the point with `π''`.
(d) Is the firm profitable there? Give the number — and if the answer surprises you, say in one line what it means for the decision. (Maximising profit and making a profit are not the same thing.)

**H4.** `f(q) = q³ − 9q² + 24q` on `[0, 6]`.
(a) Stationary points and their nature. (b) Inflection point. (c) Sketch.

## Part 2 — Board report (400–500 words, typed)

**This is a rehearsal of the midterm.** A firm has a cost function `C(q) = 0.2q² + 4q + 80` and a revenue function `R(q) = 40 ln(q + 1)`, both in € thousand per month.

Write a short report for the Board that:

1. States the profit function and studies it: domain, sign, the quantity that maximises it, and proof that it is a maximum.
2. Includes a **sketch** of the profit function, hand-drawn, with axes labelled and the maximum marked.
3. Answers this question in plain English: **at the optimum, how much room for error does the firm have?** Support it with the profit at the optimum and at one quantity either side.
4. Ends with a recommendation of one sentence.

Structure it with headings. Numbers in the text, working in an appendix. The midterm asks for a longer version of exactly this, with an added qualitative section and Harvard referencing — which we brief next.

## Self-check before submitting

- The study follows the seven steps in order, and each one is labelled.
- `π''` is computed and its **sign** is stated, not just the formula: that is what proves maximum or minimum.
- The sketch has: both axes labelled, the zeros, the maximum, and the vertical intercept.

## How this feeds the assessments

- **H1 and Part 2** are the midterm: study a function, sketch it, tell a Board what it means.
- **H2 and H3** are the resit's "full function study" question, which uses an average-cost or a log-revenue firm.
